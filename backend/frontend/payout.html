How settlements work

Instant Verification

When you add a new payout account, Payla verifies it instantly to ensure the money goes to the right person.

Next Working Day (T+1)

Payments are automatically settled to your bank account the next working day. If a client pays today, the money arrives the next working day.

Weekends & Holidays

Transfers are not processed on weekends or public holidays. Payments made during these times will be processed the next working day.
