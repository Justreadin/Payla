FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Payla free?

Yes. You can claim your personal Payla username and link without paying a subscription fee. Your link becomes functional for payments after you add your name and receiving bank details to activate it.

Do I need to subscribe to receive payments?

No. You can use the free plan to receive payments through your personal Payla link. Silver adds branded invoices, Layla's automated reminders, payment tracking, analytics, receipts, and other payment-management features.

What does Layla do?

Layla sends automated reminders about outstanding payments on your behalf through supported channels, including WhatsApp, SMS, and email. She handles reminders; she does not answer clients' questions or manage conversations.

Can I customise Layla's messages?

Yes. You can use Payla's default reminder template or write your own custom reminder message. Your custom message is used across the supported reminder channels.

What analytics does Silver include?

Silver includes payment analytics and paylink analytics. Payment analytics relates to payment activity, while paylink analytics relates to the performance of your payment page.

What's the difference between payment history and payment tracking?

Payment history gives you a record of payments you've received, available to every account through your payouts page. Silver's payment tracking helps you keep track of outstanding and paid invoices.

Can my clients pay directly from an invoice?

Yes. Silver invoices include a direct payment option, so clients can pay from the invoice.

Does Payla hold my money?

No. Payments are processed through Paystack and sent to your connected bank account. Payla does not hold your funds.

What happens when an invoice is paid?

Once successful payment is recorded, Payla automatically closes the invoice. On Silver, Payla also issues a branded PDF receipt, for you and for your client.

What does Silver cost?

Silver costs ₦7,500 per month or ₦75,000 per year. The annual plan saves ₦15,000 compared with paying monthly for a full year.

What is the founding offer?

The first 200 customers who upgrade to Silver lock in the founding price. Your founding price stays with your account. If you cancel and later resubscribe, you keep the founding rate.

What if I never upgrade?

You can continue using the free plan. You don't need to subscribe to keep your free payment link.

Are there transaction fees?

Payla charges no transaction fees. Paystack's processing fees apply at checkout, and you choose whether you or your client pays them.

Can I use a payla.vip link?

Yes, on Silver. You can choose payla.vip/@username or payla.ng/@username as your primary link. The one you choose is the canonical URL; the other redirects to it, so both addresses always work. If you later cancel Silver, your payla.vip link redirects to your payla.ng link, so anything you've already shared keeps working.
